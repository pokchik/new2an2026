>> Manuscript Submission

Submit Your Paper to NEW2AN 2026
Authors are invited to submit original, high-quality research papers presenting significant theoretical, experimental, or practical contributions in the areas covered by NEW2AN 2026. All manuscripts must be submitted electronically through the official Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit (CMT).

The submission link opens in a new browser window.
Submission Requirements
Language All submissions must be written in English.
File Format Only PDF files will be accepted for peer review.
Paper Length The maximum length is 15 pages, including figures, tables, appendices, and references, unless otherwise specified by the Program Chairs.
Formatting Manuscripts must be prepared using the official Springer Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS) format.
Submission System Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit (CMT)
Originality and Publication Policy

Submitted papers must present original and unpublished work. A manuscript must not contain plagiarized material and must not be simultaneously submitted to, or under review by, another conference, journal, or publication venue.

Submissions that violate these requirements may be rejected without review. An accepted paper may also be withdrawn from the proceedings if a violation is identified after acceptance.

Authors are responsible for ensuring that their manuscripts satisfy all originality, ethical, formatting, and publication requirements.

Author Information and Manuscript Details

The paper title and author list shown in the submitted PDF must exactly match the information entered in the Microsoft CMT submission record. All co-authors must be included in the online submission.

Authors should carefully verify names, affiliations, email addresses, and the paper title before the submission deadline.

The author list of an accepted paper may not be changed except in exceptional circumstances and with prior approval from the Program Chairs. Failure to comply with these requirements may result in withdrawal of the paper from the review or publication process.

Requirements for Accepted Papers
  • At least one author of each accepted paper must register for NEW2AN 2026 and present the paper at the conference.
  • At least one full author registration is required for every accepted paper included in the proceedings.
  • One full registration may cover up to two accepted papers presented by the same registered author.
  • Registration fees are non-refundable and must be paid before submission of the final camera-ready manuscript.
  • Camera-ready papers must incorporate the reviewers' recommendations and comply fully with Springer LNCS publication requirements.
Conference Proceedings: Accepted and presented papers will be published in the Springer Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS) , subject to final approval and compliance with Springer's editorial and publication requirements.
