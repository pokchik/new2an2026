Call for Papers — NEW2AN 2026
The 26th International Conference on Next Generation Wired/Wireless Networks and Systems (NEW2AN 2026) invites researchers, practitioners, industry professionals, and students to submit original and high-quality research papers presenting significant theoretical, experimental, or practical contributions.

Conference Dates 15–17 December 2026
Venue Colombo, Sri Lanka
Proceedings Springer Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS)
Historical Acceptance Rate Below 35% over the past five years
Publication and Indexing

The NEW2AN 2026 proceedings are confirmed for publication in the Springer Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS) series.

Springer LNCS proceedings are generally submitted for indexing or inclusion in major scholarly databases and discovery services, including Web of Science, EI Compendex, Inspec, Scopus, ACM Digital Library, DBLP, and Google Scholar, subject to the policies and acceptance procedures of the respective services.

Scope of Contributions
NEW2AN 2026 welcomes original contributions addressing emerging developments in next-generation wired and wireless networking, communication systems, architectures, applications, services, security, performance evaluation, and related technologies.

Submissions may present new theoretical results, experimental studies, system designs, prototypes, industrial case studies, or practical solutions relevant to the conference scope.
Author Guidance
  • Papers must present original and unpublished work.
  • Manuscripts must not be simultaneously submitted to another conference, journal, or publication venue.
  • Submissions must be prepared in English and follow the Springer LNCS formatting requirements.
  • All papers will undergo peer review.
  • At least one author of each accepted paper must register for and present the paper at NEW2AN 2026.
