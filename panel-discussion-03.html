Industrial Forum Panel Discussion 03 — Digital Transformation Through AI

IEEE SPS Sri Lanka Chapter Presents
Panel Discussion
Digital Transformation Through AI: Innovation, Leadership, and Responsible Adoption
Date 16 December 2026
Time 2:00 p.m. – 3:00 p.m.
Coordinated by:
IEEE Signal Processing Society Sri Lanka Chapter

About the Panel Discussion

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is redefining digital transformation across industries, enabling organizations to improve operational efficiency, enhance customer experiences, optimize decision-making, and develop innovative products and services. As AI technologies continue to mature, organizations must also address critical challenges related to governance, workforce readiness, cybersecurity, privacy, and regulatory compliance to ensure responsible and sustainable adoption.

This panel brings together senior industry leaders from the telecommunications sector, a technology entrepreneur specializing in digital transformation, and an expert in AI governance and data protection. The discussion will provide diverse perspectives on how organizations are leveraging AI to accelerate digital transformation while balancing innovation with ethical, legal, and regulatory responsibilities.

The panelists will share practical experiences and insights on topics including AI adoption in telecommunications, enterprise digital transformation strategies, workforce upskilling and organizational change, emerging business opportunities, and the evolving landscape of AI governance, data protection, and regulatory compliance. The discussion will also explore how organizations can build trust in AI systems while fostering innovation and maintaining competitiveness in an increasingly digital economy.

This panel aims to provide valuable insights for researchers, industry professionals, business leaders, policymakers, and students who are interested in understanding the strategic role of AI in digital transformation and the key considerations for its successful and responsible implementation.
