Workshop Overview

Discover How Machines Interpret the Visual World

Computer vision enables machines to interpret and analyse visual information obtained from images and videos. Powered by deep learning, it has transformed applications across healthcare, autonomous driving, security, object detection, image segmentation and three-dimensional scene understanding.

This pre-conference workshop introduces the fundamentals of neural networks and Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs). It also explores contemporary computer vision techniques, including generative models and 3D reconstruction.

Participants will additionally examine practical topics such as transfer learning, data augmentation and the limitations, robustness challenges and ethical considerations associated with AI-based vision systems.

The session combines conceptual foundations with practical insights, making it suitable for both newcomers and participants seeking to strengthen their knowledge of modern computer vision.

Learning Outcomes

Key Workshop Objectives

The workshop is designed to build foundational understanding while introducing practical approaches and current developments in computer vision.

01

Understand Deep Learning Fundamentals

Develop an understanding of the fundamental principles of deep learning and neural networks.

02

Learn How CNNs Process Images

Explore how Convolutional Neural Networks support image classification, object detection and image segmentation.

03

Explore Modern Vision Techniques

Examine generative models, self-supervised learning and three-dimensional scene reconstruction.

04

Develop Practical AI Skills

Gain practical knowledge of data preparation, data augmentation, transfer learning and model evaluation.

05

Connect Knowledge to Applications

Understand how deep-learning-based vision systems are applied in healthcare, security, autonomous driving and other fields.

06

Evaluate Challenges and Ethics

Consider the limitations, robustness challenges and ethical implications of artificial-intelligence-based computer vision.
