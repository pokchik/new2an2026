>> About the conference

 

26th International Conference on Next Generation Wired/Wireless Networks and Systems (Springer)
15–17 December 2026 // Colombo, Sri Lanka
Human Intelligence in the Age of Artificial Intelligence



Welcome to the 26th International Conference on Next Generation Wired/Wireless Networks and Systems (NEW2AN 2026)..

NEW2AN 2026 will be held in Colombo, Sri Lanka, from 15-17 December 2026,strong> bringing together researchers, academics, industry experts, and practitioners working in the areas of next-generation wired and wireless networks, communication systems, emerging network technologies, and related applications.

The conference provides an international forum for the exchange of new ideas, research results, and practical experiences in the design, development, deployment, and evaluation of advanced networking systems. NEW2AN continues its tradition of supporting high-quality technical contributions and fostering collaboration among the global research community.

The history of NEW2AN dates back to 1993. Over the years, it has become an established international conference, with proceedings published in Springer Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS).strong> The proceedings of NEW2AN 2026 are approved for publication in Springer LNCSstrong> and will be indexed by relevant databases.

The peer-review process for NEW2AN 2026 will be managed through the Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit (CMT)strong>. The Microsoft CMT service is provided free of charge by Microsoft, including support for Azure cloud services, software development, and system maintenance.

We warmly invite authors, researchers, and participants from around the world to join us in Colombo for NEW2AN 2026.
