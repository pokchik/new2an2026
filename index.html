>> About the conference

 

Conference will be hosted in Colombo,Sri Lanka, December 15-17, 2026

Welcome to the 26th International Conference on Next Generation Wired/Wireless Networks and Systems NEW2AN 2026 proceedings will be published in LNCS, Springer (approved) and indexed by relevant databases.

The history of NEW2AN dates back to 1993, today it is an established conference featured by proceedings published by Springer LNCS and distinguished keynote speakers.
