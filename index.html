26th International Conference on Next Generation Wired/Wireless Networks and Systems
15–17 December 2026  |  Colombo, Sri Lanka
Human Intelligence in the Age of Artificial Intelligence
An international forum advancing research and innovation in next-generation communication networks and intelligent systems.

Welcome to the 26th International Conference on Next Generation Wired/Wireless Networks and Systems (NEW2AN 2026).

NEW2AN 2026 will be held in Colombo, Sri Lanka, from 15–17 December 2026 , bringing together leading researchers, academics, industry professionals, and practitioners from around the world to exchange innovative ideas and recent advances in next-generation wired and wireless networks, communication systems, emerging technologies, and intelligent networking applications.

As one of the longest-running international conferences in this field, NEW2AN provides a distinguished platform for presenting original research, sharing practical experiences, fostering international collaboration, and discussing future directions in communication and networking technologies.

Established in 1993, NEW2AN has built a strong international reputation for scientific excellence. The conference proceedings are published in the prestigious Springer Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS) series. The proceedings of NEW2AN 2026 have been approved for publication in Springer LNCS, providing broad international visibility and indexing through relevant scholarly databases.

The peer-review process for NEW2AN 2026 will be conducted through the Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit (CMT) , providing authors with an efficient, transparent, and internationally recognized submission and review platform.

We warmly invite researchers, engineers, industry experts, practitioners, and graduate students from across the globe to participate in NEW2AN 2026 and experience an inspiring technical program in the vibrant city of Colombo, Sri Lanka.
